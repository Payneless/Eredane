We meet with [[Rector Darrin Holt]] to discuss his position. The council is moving against him, seeking to remove him from power.

**Holt's Offer:**

**Holt's Assessment:**

- He has done his best to instill what he's learned in the students

### Elara's Commitment

Elara commits to discovering everyone who has done wrong—those with seeds of corruption—and bringing them toward redemption.

**Holt's Warning:**

> "Do not be disillusioned."

## Zephyr's Counsel

### A Divine Encounter

While Elara patrols an open vista, a figure materializes—**Zephyr** herself, making her first direct appearance since Elara's oath.

**Zephyr's Purpose:**

1. To finally speak with Elara directly
2. To acknowledge what she perceives in her
3. To offer advice and warning

### The Divine Message

> "There are many things happening in this world, specifically around yourself. You have, in a way, dedicated yourself to me, taken steps down a path. I see something in you, something I don't see in others in quite some time. I do not wish to thrust too much upon you, but I am keeping my eye out."

### On Following the Wind

> "If you are to follow the wind, dedicate yourself to the ever-flowing path of the air around you, one must not be too stern. One must be willing to flow, take things as they may, make quick judgements, but willing to change direction at any time."

**Warning Against Rigidity:**

> "Conviction is good, but don't let that conviction become stops. There are those around you I would see as opposed or in defiance of ideologies that you hold. While my domain may be within nature, all must follow fate. Do not let your actions hinder the fate of others. Instead, let your actions embolden them. Should their thread come to an end, strike swiftly."

**Zephyr's Request:**

> "I ask you to be open to change as you ask of me. There are times a hurricane must destroy that which is solidly planted. If that hurricane were to exist at all times, there would be nothing left. Sometimes a gentle wind, slight guidance, an offer of choice, is better."

### On Allies and Enemies

> "Those around you may seem strange at times. They may make decisions or follow paths that seem at odds with yours. But where one might diverge, you might also rejoin. While I cannot say much, do not fear what is happening with your friend [[Atasche]]. While time might be required to understand, a strong ally he will be. But by that same virtue, should resistance be met, an even stronger enemy."

**Elara's Decision:** Preserve the compass for later days' activities.

> "What are you talking about, leave me alone. I don't like being stuck in here. Someone put me in here."

This confirms Atasche is conscious and aware of confinement, not possessed.
