
# Gods in [[Marodae]]

**Date:** 1/25/2026  
**In-Game Date:** 2nd Mischra 2187  
**Start Location:** Road outside Tiranos  
**End Location:** [[Marodae]]  
**Objective:** Travel to [[Marodae]] and settle in

---

## The Journey to [[Marodae]]

The party travels south by cart toward [[Marodae]]. **Five days of travel lie ahead.**

### Day 1: The Library Discovery

[[Laogys]] discovered his necromantic tome from a mysterious "library" containing graves and a tomb. Inside the tomb sits a box alongside the book.

**Critical Finding:** [[Laogys]] did not know any magic before acquiring this book.

**Ominous Detail:** All other kobolds [[Laogys]] knew "exploded."

### The Mirror Interrogation

[[Elara Sunstride|Elara]] discusses using the mirror with [[Ronan]] then directs it toward [[Laogys]].

**Mirror Reveals:**
- A faint shadow over [[Laogys]], emanating from the book (similar in nature to the shadow around [[Richard]])
- A faint aura from the book itself, indicating the object does not belong to its owner

**Lesson Learned:** [[Alice]] warns the group about upcasting and its dangers.

### Evening Camp

The group makes camp. [[Alice]] approaches [[Elara Sunstride|Elara]] with concerns.

---

## Alice's Concerns

### The Question of Pursuit

[[Alice]] asks if the party is being hunted. **[[Elara Sunstride|Elara]] fails to lie convincingly.**

### Alexander's Staff

[[Alice]] brings up her staff, a gift from her master **Alexander**. She knew it was powerful but has no knowledge of its origin.

---

## Five Days Pass

### Day 1
Nothing happens. Mirror directed at [[Zennick]] reveals nothing unusual.

### Day 2
Nothing happens. Mirror directed at [[Kaz]] reveals a slight disturbance, she doesn't belong on this plane.

### Day 3: The Wagon Attack
A wagon is discovered damaged on the side of the road, with dead bodies inside. **Attack occurred within the last day.** Arrows embedded in the wagon's side suggest a recent bandit attack.

**Critical Discovery:** [[Alice]] finds a small wooden box at the back of the wagon containing a **Spell Scroll of Hold Person**.

Mirror directed at [[Atasche]] reveals **magical influence, inverted**.

### Day 4
Nothing happens. Mirror directed at [[Alice]] reveals her staff does not belong to her.

### Day 5: The Revelation
Mirror directed at [[Ronan]] reveals **three things**:
- The sword does not belong to him
- Definitely magical influence present
- An illusion over [[Ronan]], which when revealed shows him to be thinner, more gaunt, a normal man beneath the facade

**[[Ronan]]'s Relief:** [[Ronan]] seems relieved by the revelation.

---

## Arrival in [[Marodae]]

The party arrives at **[[Marodae]]**, a city ringed with stone walls and buildings constructed from white stone quarried from nearby mountains.

**Population:** Approximately 20,000 people

**Notable Features:**
- Multiple larger buildings serving as arenas and playhouses
- [[King & Cask]], a tavern and inn where [[Richard]] leads the party

---

## King & Cask Tavern and Inn

### The Proprietor

A young female dwarf runs the establishment.

### Evening Performance

[[Zennick]] and [[Richard]] perform while the party settles in. [[Elara Sunstride|Elara]] finishes her meal and uses **Divine Sense**.

**Divine Sense Reveals:**
- The inn is consecrated ground
- Two people at a nearby table playing dice possess strong celestial aura **they are gods**

### The Dice Game Participants

**First Participant (Woman):** Mid-30s, long red hair, wearing traveler's clothes with an overcoat

**Second Participant (Man):** Mid-30s, dressed in fine regalia, appears to be an actor

### The Fight and the Third God

A fight breaks out over the dice game. A third figure enters, wearing a cloak. Their eyes flash with power, instantly stopping the fight.

The two original gods embrace and resume their game. The third god joins their table.

## Ronin's Blade Origin

### The Mystery of the Sword

**Discovery:** [[Ronan]]'s sword was left in his cart by someone he doesn't know. The gift was deliberate; the giver disappeared into the night after leaving it.

**What Lives Inside the Sword:** Every previous user who has ever wielded the blade resides within it as voices/spirits.

**The Blade's Nature:** The previous users share their opinions freely about current wielders, commenting on whether owners are good or bad, and whether they recognize anything familiar.

### Ronin's Resistance

[[Ronan]] worked in a bar in **Dovisar** before this journey. He expresses reluctance to follow the purpose the sword demands of him.

---

## Richard's Update and Fortuna's Summons

[[Richard]] arrives with information that [[Fortuna]] wishes to meet with [[Elara Sunstride|Elara]].

**[[Ronan]]'s Request:** [[Ronan]] asks to accompany [[Elara Sunstride|Elara]] to the meeting.

**Meeting Location:** A grove located 4 blocks away from the tavern. [[Richard]] does not specify the exact time.

### Richard, Kaz, and Zennick

The three engage in a conversation about their respective pasts. **[[Kaz]] refuses to elaborate on why she was removed from the fae court** because **[[Zennick]] does not speak to his own matters.** The conversation stalls without resolution.

## Meeting with Fortuna

[[Elara Sunstride|Elara]] and [[Ronan]] make their way to the grove specified by [[Fortuna]].

### Fortuna's Questions and Revelations

#### On Tamuel Markath's Quest

[[Fortuna]] asks: "Did Tamuel find what he was looking for?"

[[Elara Sunstride|Elara]] responds: He did not.

#### On Fate and Destiny

[[Fortuna]]: "Fate has an interesting pull around you all."

She expresses interest in understanding why the party has arrived now, at this moment, in [[Marodae]].

#### On the Blade of Fate

[[Fortuna]] reveals that the blade [[Ronan]] carries has a storied past and **chose Ronin for a reason**.

**The Blade's Many Names:**
- Known by several different names throughout history
- Associated with: [[Zephyr]], **[[Agrasyn]]**, **[[Umbris]]**, and the **[[The Weaver]]**
- **Primary Name:** The Blade of Fate

#### On Eastern Tensions

[[Fortuna]] warns of mounting tensions to the east where "the empire grows aggressive."

**Critical Warning:** "Things will begin to shift soon. The impact will be far-reaching than expected."

**Binding Oath:** A contract is made, the party agrees not to speak publicly about this matter.

---

## The Gods' Mistake and Divine Binding

> "The gods made a mistake. Without going into too much detail, as the knowledge would be... problematic, we, in binding ourselves to mortals, are bound a little too closely. As you know, the gods have their domains, powers, their specialties. However, much of that is influenced by perception. As perceptions shift and change, as ideologies and desires shift and change, so too do the gods associated with them. Should that knowledge become widely known, it could be used against us. I can't say much more than that, but you should keep this close to your heart. There are things happening which that is pertinent.
>
> One example: myself and [[Iena]] are two sides of the same coin. People often confuse us, and sometimes that becomes problematic. I myself am comfortable with it at times; [[Iena]] hates it. She's an enchantress in all forms of the word. I myself am more innocent. The path you all are on, as Fortuna sees it, leads toward places where that information will be useful."

---

## Fortuna's Final Warning

> "There are obstacles in our path that we must overcome. Hoping this information provides use for those, I can't say more, things are still a bit foggy. Outside of that, the other thing I can say: **keep your friends close**. There are trials ahead that each of you will face, and without the others, they can end in ruinous disaster. Your mentor was searching for something that even the gods don't have an answer to. I get the feeling something linked to that will provide more answers. I don't know if they will be good ones, but perhaps some insight."
