## Morning - Elara and Zennick

- Elara meets with [[Zennick]] to ask about his opinions on the group:
  - Thinks [[Ronan]] is a good guy, but the sword causes him anxiety
  - Appreciates [[Laogys]]'s open-mindedness
  - Doesn't see life's value the same way [[Richard]] does, but likes that he appreciates the arts
  - Isn't confident any of them have enough knowledge about [[Atasche]]'s situation
- Elara raises the topic of multiple party members getting tied to necromancy - does it bother him?
- Elara asks if there's a specific deity Zennick finds interesting or wishes to follow:
  - **[[Tinker| The Tinkerer]]** - a craftsman deity who interacted with him in a dream
  - The Tinkerer knew about Zennick's creator and others around, but none up to Zennick's sophistication
  - The capital of the empire, far east, creates robots for destruction
  - Zennick wants to explore other deities but worries about the danger they could bring to the group
- Elara asks about the lights at the Prism - Zennick will keep it in mind but has no further thoughts

## Training and Departure Plans

- [[Elara Sunstride]] heads out to train; [[Atasche]] and [[Richard]] are there, Richard interested in learning martial combat
- Elara plans to train, pick up rations, and set out shortly after noon
- Discusses with [[Ronan]] about being involved with the Sable Dominion and what to do about them

## The Letter and the Town Crier

- A letter is delivered to Elara - invitation to appear at the city offices to be thanked for services provided
- A town crier announces in the distance: "Blades in the night, Lyceum in morning" - the rector and others displayed in terror, attacks following numerous mysterious events at the academy
  - No names given; rector killed, murdered in their sleep, along with staff and students
- [[Ronan]] gives a beggar 8 silver and 8 copper; the beggar says: *"They're watching. The collective needs you. The inquisition is at hand."*
  - Claims to be a mere footsoldier; calls someone else "a true inquisitor"
- Ronan notes that [[Atasche]] and [[Richard]] haven't arrived yet

## Atasche and Richard's Interrogation

- Atasche and Richard were grabbed - bags over their heads - and interrogated by a mob boss in the city
- Not connected to the Sable Dominion culprit from the previous night
- They had reach in other cities and were curious if the party had connections with the Sable Dominion
- Elara and the group find them in a town square; they say "talk about it later, we're okay"

## The Administration Offices

- Party receives information about the poison: specifically harms plants, a new poison
- Extended a personal invitation to tonight's gala - the city holds a harvest celebration
  - Gala at **House Aerthyan** at 6
  - No further info on the Lyceum attack
- Reward: **100 gold** and a **staff from a vineyard** (bundle of grapes at the end)

## Preparation for Travel

- Plan: 16 days across the desert, then to [[Namaris]]
- Purchase 50 rations, 4 replacement wheels for travel
- Purchase fine clothes for Elara, Laogys, and Richard

## The Gala at House Aerthyan

- **Lord Aerthyan** thanks the party; they've had previous encounters with trouble but nothing to this level
- Elara works the room, meeting heads of noble families
- Advice given: many wish to advance their positions, making connections is important

**Noble Houses:**
- **House Fareth** - seems familiar with Richard and Atasche; says as long as no one interferes with their business
- **House Lysmere** - acquaintance made
- **House Brennol** - acquaintance made
- **[[Misha]]** - less tied to houses, more tied to the city itself; protector of the city, follower of Flora
  - Certain unnamed organizations have not acted like this before; the fact they're acting now is confusing and concerning, especially alongside the Lyceum events
  - Suggests a meeting of **the Concordium** may be in order
  - If the council was involved, that could be connected - seems strange unless certain unnamed parties are involved

- [[Laogys]] is proactive in talking to others
- Elara casts Divine Sense - detects a **strong celestial presence** from a taller woman, somewhat uncomfortable in fine clothes, long blonde hair, piercing green eyes
- [[Ronan]] asks Elara to dance; they dance while Richard, Laogys, and Zennick gather at the punch bowl

## The Side Room

- Richard, Laogys, and Zennick are invited into a side room
- Elara and Ronan locate them afterward
- Elara cures Richard and Laogys of "influences" they had in them

## The Award and the Attack

- Party gathers to receive the award from Lord Aerthyan as he presents to the crowd
- As the award is about to be presented, **explosions go off throughout the ballroom** - large enough to take people out
- Attackers draw blades and begin hacking people around them
- **Lord Aerthyan is stabbed in the neck**
- Lord Fareth is escorted out; Lord Brennol has fallen, guards around him; House Lysmere is not in sight
- [[Richard]] heals House Brennol's head as the party attempts to evacuate
- Elara tackles the nearest attacker, reaches into their mouth to prevent suicide, knocks them out, and carries them out for questioning
- The party narrowly escapes as the manor begins to burn, seemingly getting all the Lords out

## The Sky Darkens

- The sky above darkens and swirls, descending toward the manor
- Lights begin to float up from the house
- **Souls are being siphoned**
