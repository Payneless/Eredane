## Session 21 — _The Long Road to Juhara_

**Date:** 5/31/2026 
**In-Game Date:** 32nd of Mishra, 2187 (spans ~20 days of travel) 
**Start Location:** [[Avenne]] 
**Objective:** Travel to Juhara — 20 days until arrival

---

### Summary

A travel montage from Avenne to the fortress city of Juhara. Strange roadside encounters mark each day, and a campfire honesty session finally airs the party's divine connections and hidden pasts.

---

### Items Found

- **Blueberry alcoholic drink** — gift from a drunk traveler; grants **water breathing**
- **Coin pouch** — 6 gold, 10 silver, with note: _"Return to Wilhelm in Corville's Gate."_ Party plans to post it from the next tower

---

### Key Events

#### Leaving Avenne

- A drunk man loses his coin pouch on the road; Elara alerts him, and he thanks her with the blueberry water-breathing drink
- A herd of beasts — anteater/bison crosses — blocks the fields ahead; they back off as Elara approaches on her steed

---

#### The Headless Man

- In a large forest, a headless body wanders searching for its head — which is talking to it
- Elara calls Richard over as the possible expert
- The man refuses to say how he lost his head ("just out walking"); the head won't confirm whether the body is originally his
- Kaz reduces the size of the head

---

#### The Wizard Duel

- Three figures in matching gray robes in a cleared field — two dueling back to back with wands, one officiating
- Magic missile vs. shield; they start arguing
- Zennick and Kaz dispel their shields — round called a draw, they go again
- Zennick, Kaz, Laogys, and Richard interfere before being told off

---

#### The Adventurer Camp

Four adventurers setting up camp:

|Member|Description|
|---|---|
|Goliath|Large axe|
|Young elf|Leathers, green cloak and hood|
|Mage|Robes|
|Swordsman|Sword on hip|

- Elara asks to share camp — accepted
- Laogys asks to spar; Elara asks if any serve a divine power — none do
- They grew up together in the same small village
- Elara mentions the tragedy at [[Vassalor]] but doesn't place the party there

---

#### Roadside Vignettes (one per day)

- **Kobold & orc travelers** — discussing prejudice against monstrous folk "with adventurers about"; the orc asks if the party is viewed as different; Zennick asks the kobold about building larger devices
- **Troll in the fruit grove** — large, ugly, at the base of the trees; Zennick prioritizes safety over saving the trees; party passes on
- **The noble's gift** — a well-dressed noble with guards and servants waylays the party: he's about to propose and wants a gift; interested in the **teapot of tea time** and the **figure of cat summoning**
- **The bear** — _"I beg your finest pardon, would you mind helping me? The tree over there serves a delectable syrup, but I can't seem to get it down"_
- **Wilhelm's pouch** — found in the middle of the road (see Items Found)
- **Broken cart** — party helps a man lift and repair it
- **The bugbear and the desk** — a tie-wearing bugbear cursing at an office desk that spits paper forms at him; nothing can be done, destroying it doesn't work
- **Centaur herd** — crosses the road ahead
- **The windmill** — one man trying to slay another inside the windmill, but only hitting the windmill; a fan bumps the old man
- **The manticore** — circling overhead, killed by multiple lightning strikes; **8 mages in gray and blue robes** emerge and circle the body
- **Entering the desert** — party changes into desert clothes; a happy half-orc woman passes reciting awful poetry; party reaches the **Mahjon River**, which flows north to the cities
- **Tabaxi musician** — brownish-tan fur, asks to play a song for the party
- **The tortle** — riding a chair floating an inch above the river as boats pass
- **Halflings vs. dwarf** — halflings with pitchforks fending off a naked one-armed dwarf; Zennick portals him elsewhere

---

#### Campfire Honesty Session

Ronan raises possible accusations of hiding things from the party; Laogys references the old man's warning in Avenne. Ronan asks everyone to be clear about their god connections:

|Character|Revelation|
|---|---|
|**Laogys**|His book is **Laogys's Grimoire**; his real name was "Great Pack Leader" — the arresting guards named him Laogys; his figure may be tied to [[Agrasyn]]|
|**Kaz**|Assumes it was **Shadall** who spoke to her, given the Void connection; would return to her Faerie Court if she could|
|**Ronan**|Made contact with **[[Akmera]]**; parents worked as cobblers; lived in Dovisar his whole life|
|**Zennick**|Has dreamt of **the Tinkerer**|
|**Elara**|Reveals her parents are from **[[Qara'Sul]]**|

---

#### Arrival at Juhara

- Evening: the party sees **Juhara** — a fortress city of large sandstone, the only bridge over the river
- Sunlight gleams off glass and metal roofs and windows
- **The party is finally within the [[Skuviri Dominion]]**
