# Traveling to Avenne

**Date:** 3/1/2026
**In-Game Date:** 12th Mischra
**Start Location:** [[Marodae]]
**End Location:** Outskirts of [[Avenne]]
**Objective:** Travel south to [[Avenne]], then east toward [[Namaris]]

**Key NPCs:** Plain-looking gentleman (memory agent), Yuan-ti (deceased), Necromancer traveler (student of Master Icarus)

**Notable Items:** Enhanced Staff of the Python (left in yuan-ti temple)

**Unresolved:** Identity/organization of memory agents, yuan-ti temple and its significance, necromancer traveler's purpose, Harvest Festival in Avenne

---

**The Deal**

- A plain-looking gentleman enters and drops his hood
- Offers the party the same deal as before: memory removal, but [[Atasche]] won't be taken - just ensure news doesn't spread
- [[Atasche]] asks about the other two being returned; confirmed if deal is taken
- [[Elara Sunstride|Elara]] whispers a prayer to Zephyr on the wind: *"Stay away from Marodae"*
- Memories altered - replaced with mundane days
- [[Atasche]] begins fabricating stories during the blank period, gets caught, causes minor annoyance
- Party wakes up in camp south of the city with no memory of anyone going missing

**Destination Debate**

- [[Atasche]] wants to go somewhere [[Elara Sunstride|Elara]] trusts more
- [[Elara Sunstride|Elara]] suggests heading toward Vassalor
- Desert to the east ruled out - insufficient rations
- Vassalor means backtracking - unsatisfactory
- Party decides: south to [[Avenne]], then east to [[Namaris]]

---

**Day 1** - No incidents

**Day 2** - Storm curling around the mountains

- [[Zennick]] notices a twinkle in the distance - a couple miles up the mountain
- [[Atasche]], [[Zennick]], and [[Kaz]] go to investigate; the rest set up camp
- Grows more clearly a light source as they approach
- Partly damaged yuan-ti statue on the way up; signs of recent battle - massive claw marks, scorched and melted cave walls
- A severed leg visible through the entrance; glow emanates from inside
- An ornately carved wooden staff with a serpent head; figures with backs turned to the group
- Group returns to inform the party

- [[Elara Sunstride|Elara]] notes most yuan-ti tribes would be criminals or threats - they worship the Great Serpent
- Party debates; only one yuan-ti appears able to move
- [[Ronan]] and [[Zennick]] stay behind with Alice; the rest venture up

- [[Richard]] says *"we come in peace"* - the yuan-ti charges
- [[Laogys]] blasts it; life leaves its eyes
- [[Richard]] grabs the staff, which appears to absorb something from the dead yuan-ti
- Temple interior features a giant serpent image across the back wall
- Assessment: they were defending the temple - possibly the staff - and something had attacked; acting in self-defense, one survivor remained
- [[Richard]] decides to leave the staff behind in the temple
- Staff identified as an enhanced Staff of the Python with extra magical power

- [[Elara Sunstride|Elara]] goes to bed frustrated - [[Laogys]] eliminated any chance to speak with the near-dead yuan-ti, and [[Richard]]'s interest in the staff felt self-serving

**Day 3** - Nothing of note

**Day 4** - Drizzle; a wagon in the distance

- [[Atasche]] notices a broken wheel and figures meandering near it
- At ~120 ft - undead shuffling around the wagon
- [[Elara Sunstride|Elara]] and [[Ronan]] charge; another figure emerges from behind the wagon - *"Please wait!"*
- She's not from anywhere in particular, just wanders
- Trying to repair the wheel; undead helping lift it
- Studied under a **Master Icarus**; he sent her out to experience the world and learn what he couldn't teach
- Mentions a **Harvest Festival** in [[Avenne]]

**Day 5** - Farmland thickens as the party nears [[Avenne]]

- Agricultural heart of the League
- Two large lakes visible on either side of the city
