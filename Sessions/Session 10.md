# The Underground Confrontation

**Date:** 2/8/2026  
**In-Game Date:** 11th Mischra 2187  
**Start Location:** [[Marodae]] (inn)  
**End Location:** Boarded building / Underground entrance  
**Objective:** Escape city / investigate memory manipulation

**Key NPCs:** The Figure (memory manipulator), Agrasyn (Richard's deal), cultist leader with tentacles, hooded cultists, band member (more aware)

**Notable Items:** Liquid wax (ear plugs), Hunter's Mark placed on figure

**Unresolved:** Nature of "the song," underground location, cultist organization, Richard's deal with Agrasyn, Elara/Richard confrontation, Atasche's "other self"

---

**The Threat**

- Mysterious figure appears, identifies Atasche as problem
- Offers: return friends OR party kills Atasche
- Explains: city improved through memory suppression; "the song" controls what people remember
- Party theorizes blocking hearing might prevent effects

**Preparations**

- Elara blocks ears with wax, marks figure with Hunter's Mark to track
- Kaz activates lattice to record thoughts
- Richard makes deal with Agrasyn: temporary deafness for soul cost
- Elara follows figure through city, loses trail, returns

**Planning & Investigation**

- Party communicates via paper (can't speak safely)
- Split into 3 pairs to find underground entrance
- Kaz + Laogys discover boarded building

**Combat - Cultist Leader & 4 Hooded Cultists**

Elara makes first strike. Zennick accelerates her dagger on a critical hit, the blade planting itself in the leader's side. Atasche follows with a critical spear strike, dashing over to land a vicious kick to the leader's backside.

The music pierces through—something tries to affect their minds again. The leader seems unbothered by the strikes.

Laogys roasts one cultist alive in retaliation. Richard's spell takes out another. The cultist leader barely seems bothered by how quickly his followers fall.

Richard takes a spellcast and grapple from tentacles—he's down. Laogys finishes the last cultist while Elara, Kaz, Zennick, and Atasche focus fire on the leader.

As the music swells, the wax proves useless. Elara temporarily forgets what she's doing, struggling to hold onto her plans. Zennick fires magic missiles from her palms. The leader responds with a tentacle whip—Elara falls hard despite Zennick's efforts to shield her. He stares down Atasche, landing a minor psychic blow that Atasche endures.

Atasche reaches to his "other side" and asks for him to take over—just this once. His other self tears into the leader with claws. Laogys lands a critical eldritch blast to the leader's backside. The Hex damage is too much.

A horrible screeching cry. A shadow retreats from the cultist's body as his head is bitten off.

Richard is stabilized. Elara picks herself up, walks to Richard, and lays on hands with everything she has. As he gets up, he crawls to one of the dead bodies and plunges a shadow claw into its heart, pulling a tether of light that fades away.

Elara and Richard have a heated confrontation—an argument that leaves both frustrated. The rest investigate inside the building.
