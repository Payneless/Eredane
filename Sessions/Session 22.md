

**Date:** 7/5/2026  
**In-Game Date:** 32nd of Mishra, 2187 (spans ~20 days of travel)  
**Start Location:** [[Juhara]]
**Objective:** Explore Ruins of Amphis

**1. Arrival in Juhara**

- City of faith and duty: construct sentries, siege-hardened shrines, ceremonial bells/drums.
- Guard checks in on the party (offers water); locals unaware of events in the Dovisarian League.
- Bazaar visit — bronze-plate pricing by color-coded goods, gathered supplies (cartography tools for Zennick; blankets, flute, manacles for Elara).
- Recommended stops: House of Two Lamps (inn), Well of First Light / Shrine of Eternal Pursuit (religious), Moonshade Baths (relaxation), Bronze Jackal (smithing), Hall of Songs.
- Learned: magic was gifted by Akyros; lower gods beneath him created the schools. Necromancy carries a bad reputation locally due to an undead threat.

**2. Moonshade Baths — the Overheard Argument**

- Quiet, politically-charged baths (moonlit chambers, no alcohol — tea/coffee/fruit).
- Caravan captains trade news in the courtyard each morning; Elara eavesdrops over tea.
- Half-orc and a hardier human argue over a desert expedition ("deserts are dangerous" vs. "we've wasted enough time").
- Half-orc shares a clay tablet with an old desert-language riddle marking a route.

**3. The Journey**

- Followed the riddle: east from the river, until moonset, to a canyon.
- First canyon search (aerial recon by Kaz/Laogys, detect magic) — dead end.
- Pushed south; found a dark volcanic slab (a known marker type from the Divine Era).
- Located cliffs shaped like a reclining giant, entered a narrow canyon, crossed an ancient salt flat to reach the ruins.

**4. The Ruins — Trials**

- **Mosaic Hall:** Riddle text overhead; crossed only the dark tiles to pass safely.
- **Corpse Room:** Two dead adventurers, gear scattered; carved snakes with "watching" eyes; party left belongings behind before crossing.
- **Hunter's Gaze Chamber:** Circular room, stone eyes woke mid-crossing, inducing dread ("only prey fears the hunter's gaze"). Elara pushed through using her hunting/justice mindset; Richard broke and was struck by phantom fangs.
- **Pedestal Room:** Held silence; stone jaw opened to reveal a smooth sphere. Kaz placed it on the pedestal, revealing a hidden passage.
- **Looted Antechamber:** Signs a rival party (3 individuals) arrived ~a day earlier — opened chests, empty pedestals, small blood trace, no bodies, one entrance/exit. Elara's ritual study found teleportation-circle residue and slither-trail grooves in the only snake-free mosaic, leading to a new passage.

**5. The Worship Chamber**

- 80-ft relief depicting yuan-ti history with a channel missing something.
- Richard placed a recovered staff into the matching indent — it dissolved, and the god-serpent **Amphis** manifested from the relief, thanking the party for its restoration.
- Reward room revealed: 2,000 gp, serpent scale armor, Serpent's Fang, Robe of Serpents, Dagger of Venom, and the legendary rapier **the Black Lotus**. Amphis also grants the party an ally, **Cerastes**.

**6. Session End**

- Party camps at the ruin entrance.

- **Half-orc trader/guide** — Found the clay tablet riddle; leads the party toward the ruin. Practical, desert-savvy, frustrated with his companion.
- **Human adventurer (~80 HP)** — Half-orc's companion; dismissive of desert dangers, abrasive, doesn't fully grasp what they're walking into.
- **Amphis** — Yuan-ti god-serpent, restored to physical form via the staff. Grateful, welcoming; refers to yuan-ti as "my offspring." Potential recurring divine contact.
- **Cerastes** — Ally/boon gifted by Amphis; loyalty implied, role not yet defined.
- **Rival party (unnamed, 3 individuals)** — Beat the group to the ruin by about a day; fate unknown (blood but no bodies, no visible exit).
