# Session 6: The Vault and Departure

**Date:** 1/4/2026  
**In-Game Date:** TBD  
**Start Location:** Lyceum Grounds  
**End Location:** Road outside Tiranos  
**Objective:** Investigate the mysterious letter, explore the vault, and decide the party's future

---

## Morning Errands

Find Cai Thorne outside sitting in the shade. Pickup magic robes at desk in observatory.

---

## The Embarrassing Incident

The most embarrassing, awful situation has happened, forever shaming [[Zennick]] and [[Kaz]]. [[Ronin]] saves the day.

---

## The Intercepted Letter

A letter arrives, sealed with black wax bearing a 3-pointed star.

[[Kaz]] reports that [[Liraen]] was no help, something happened she doesn't want to discuss. They found a desk they couldn't open, and a sending stone searching for an "agent."

[[Elara Sunstride|Elara]] feels she has seen the symbol before.

### Agent Echo's Orders

> The Civic Purity Council moves exactly as predicted. Their appetite for scandal exceeds their patience for proof. Continue feeding them the appearance of impropriety, not crimes, but questions.
> 
> Holt's protection of "exceptional students" must be framed as favoritism, not philosophy. Let them believe the rot is procedural. Our interest remains unchanged: control of the Lyceum requires neither siege nor spectacle, only replacement. Once Holt is removed, the Council will accept the interim rector we provide, believing the choice their own. Maintain pressure.
> 
> Agitate rival Houses. Encourage public dissent. The students will do the rest. When the lantern is extinguished, we will enter as caretakers.
> 
> The Dominion is watching.

---

## Death of Kilo Trenfall

**Kilo Trenfall**, tabaxi journalist, found dead on the ground. No sign of injury. Terrified visage frozen on her face.

[[Zennick]] starts asking for help while several party members stealth and hide.

**[[Professor Voss]]** appears and asks what happened. **Sister Arathen** glances at Voss with a confused but knowing look.

> "Whatever this was, was magic." - Professor Voss

Clutched in Kilo's hand is a piece of paper. [[Ronin]] leans down and takes it.

### The Note

Contains symbols: pillar, lion embossed, arrow, vault.

[[Rector Darrin Holt|Rector Holt]] seems to know what it means.

### Holt's Assessment

Speaking with Holt, he suspects "the Dominion" refers to the **Sable Dominion**.

[[Kaz]] asks: "Who is that?"

Voss fears for our safety.

[[Kaz]] informs Rector Holt about the journal mentioning the previous Rector's disappearance.

---

## Following the Compass

The seeker's compass starts moving, the sending stone has relocated somewhere in the direction of the sea.

We travel to where the light phenomena previously occurred, but find nothing.

### The Strange Tunnel

A tunnel leading off to the side begins to stretch and pull away... weirdly.

[[Laogys]] fires a blast into it, the spell stretches and elongates.

[[Elara Sunstride|Elara]], [[Laogys]], and [[Atasche]] approach to investigate. Nothing seems off until [[Atasche]] catches an opening in the tunnel's side.

As we learn about the entrance, we begin to see it.

### The Spiral Staircase

A spiral staircase descends into darkness.

We turn to find the group, but [[Elara Sunstride|Elara]] walks off toward the pit. [[Atasche]] barely saves her. We appear inside the tunnel, forgetting what just happened, only remembering the tunnel stretching.

[[Professor Voss]] finds us and brings us to the Rector's office.

---

## Rector Holt's Ultimatum

Holt offers to take us to the vault, then presents two options:

1. **Stay:** Continue studying at the school; he will do his best to protect us
2. **Leave:** He provides what resources he can, and we travel

He admits he doesn't feel he can protect us.

---

## Descent to the Vault

### The Fall

The party proceeds down. [[Elara Sunstride|Elara]] slips on the stairs. [[Atasche]] catches her, but **Richard** tumbles over the edge and hits the ground.

[[Ronin]] runs down while [[Kaz]] and [[Laogys]] fly.

Lay on hands eventually breathes life back into Richard, but [[Elara Sunstride|Elara]] notices something, a resistance to the healing. A foreign entity is present.

> "Richard, do you need to tell me something?" - Elara

Richard wants privacy, but there's no blood. Richard appears to not be... alive.

### The Hidden Archway

At the bottom: smooth stone walls. An archway materializes.

[[Elara Sunstride|Elara]] and [[Kaz]] walk through; others see only a physical wall.

**Discovery:** The archway only materializes when described to others or when they become aware of it.

Eventually all make it through. Thirty feet down the hall, a large black iron doorway blocks the path.

### The Runic Door

[[Elara Sunstride|Elara]] knocks, sound is deadened. No handles, no easy way to open.

[[Zennick]] observes runic language scribed in small variations across the door, something ancient.

[[Atasche]] has some familiarity but nothing concrete.

The door folds into a cube and drops into the floor.

---

## The Motes of Light

Motes of light enter each of us, different colors.

**Elara:** White

We each see our respective colors around [[Richard]] and [[Atasche]].

### Richard's Confession

> "I have a friend, down there... When I was down there, I made a deal, and I am undead. I want to protect you. What happened to my family doesn't happen to my new family. I have a personal grudge I need to take care of."

[[Elara Sunstride|Elara]] makes Richard swear on his family's name to seek to undo what he has done to himself. Then gives him a hug.

> "Everyone deserves a second chance." - Elara (under her breath)

---

## The Crystal Vision

A crystal wall stands before us.

[[Elara Sunstride|Elara]] and [[Kaz]] reach out and place their hands on the wall. A vision appears:

- A silver-lit city floating above the world
- A river of light splitting like a prism
- A woman with no face weaving a golden thread
- Nine shadows trying to break the weave from below
- A lantern between them

---

## The Vault Chamber

A chamber ahead, color kaleidoscoping across the room. A pedestal holds a small hand mirror and a geometric puzzle box.

[[Elara Sunstride|Elara]] picks up the mirror; [[Kaz]] takes the box.

### The Mirror

[[Elara Sunstride|Elara]] looks into the mirror and catches a glimpse of Richard, a sunken, hollow face, older and decrepit. Shows nothing strange for anyone else.

### The Puzzle Box

[[Kaz]] opens the box. Something anchors within her.

**Aetherglass Lens:** Reveals something out of place within 30 feet, once per day.

---

## Debriefing with Holt

We inform Rector Holt of most of what happened.

[[Elara Sunstride|Elara]] asks his best guess about the nine shadows.

> "Could be many things. Nine is strange, not sure. There are many who wish to control their own fate, many who wish to see their own destinies fulfilled. Tamuel was always concerned over the disappearance of the Oracle of Fate." - Rector Holt

---

## Departure

Preparations to leave begin, supplies and study materials gathered.

About an hour later, we exit the city onto the road elsewhere.
