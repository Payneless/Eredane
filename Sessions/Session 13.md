## Investigating the Rot

- [[Elara Sunstride|Elara]] and [[Zennick]] head to the town square
- A farmer complains about a bad crop - Elara casts Purify Food and Drink, which does remedy it
- Farmer mentions the fields near his farm are affected; party heads out to investigate
- Someone notes he's a bit of a wolf-crier, but the fields are genuinely affected - rot is spreading
- Elara identifies the source as the aqueduct coming from the city
- She purifies the water and watches to see if the rot is fast-acting
- [[Zennick]] takes off toward the city to inform the rest of the group
- The investigation is handed off to the local guard

## Festival Preparation

- Games announced for the next day: boat race, hog catch, and drinking contest
- [[Elara Sunstride|Elara]] and [[Ronan]] train together - Ronan lets Elara try his sword for a bit, but it seems to be a normal longsword in her hands
- [[Atasche]], [[Kaz]], and [[Laogys]] start their own training via a friendly competition
- Party finishes training and heads back to the room

## Festival Day - The Games

- The party signs up for the boat race
- They narrowly win the boat race
- They also succeed in the hog catching

## The Rot Returns

- Upon leaving the games, the party notices a priest talking emphatically about something
- An experimental orchard is also suffering from the rot
- Elara and the party are looped in upon approaching the priest and an official
- Elara casts Detect Poison and Disease - the ground is full of poison, only harmful to plant life, steeped in the water
- The official goes to investigate the source
- [[Richard]] detaches his hand and sends it searching for an issue - finds nothing

## Into the Cistern

- Party continues searching upstream to find an evident source
- One waterway leads to both the orchard and the field
- They find a portion that leads underground with plant-like growth on it - a hardy algae common to the area that persists through flowing water
- A slime slowly approaches; it takes damage from a spell by [[Zennick]] but stops and sits once it enters sunlight
- Oozes are reported as harmless and common to the area
- Party heads further in, about 60-70 ft - a few more slimes, shallow water flow
- It's dark; [[Kaz]] casts Dancing Lights
- They arrive at a sluice gate and find footprints at the cistern
- Footprints: average male, human, general shoes - party follows for about 10 minutes
- Elara begins tracking the individual, using her prior experience tracking criminals from her guard days
- She continues the pursuit throughout the day while the rest of the party heads back to the festival for the drinking game

## The Chase

- Elara finds someone slinking around in a trenchcoat
- She casts Message - the figure immediately runs
- Elara tackles them; there is a crunch before the figure's head blows off as Elara tries to stop it
- Elara searches the body, then walks it back to the cistern area and reconnects with [[Ronan]]
- The body has a fading illusion of a tristar-type symbol and a liquid
- Elara reports the full situation to the guards that approach
- [[Ronan]] finds a small clear sphere that apparently acts as the poison - a time-release capsule
- One guard identifies the symbol - it belongs to **The Sable Dominion**
- Elara leads the other guard back to the trail she followed
- Party heads back to the festival to reconnect
