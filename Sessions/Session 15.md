## Summary

The party flees [[Avenne]] following the manor incident, heading to [[Lord Aerthyan]]'s estate south of the city. A mysterious assassin arrives at the estate with unclear motivations, triggering a combat encounter where multiple party members transform into elemental/magical forms. The party survives, though significant questions remain about the Sable Dominion's broader agenda.

---

## Key Events

### Departure from Avenne

- Party hears lingering screams from those who didn't escape the manor
- Two survivors suggest leaving immediately
- Kaz joins the group at the tavern wagon
- Encounter **Tall blonde woman with green eyes, city noble**
- Swirling cloud visible in sky as they depart
- [[Lord Aerthyan]] proposes heading to his estate (~1 day south)

### Prisoner/Captive Situation

- Elara casts **Ceremony - Atonement** to assess alignment change
- Captive awakens but provides no answers
- **Misha reveals:** The individual is "no longer there"—memory alterations have occurred; they're not a person anymore
- Elara attempts to prevent physical harm; **Misha shuts her down**, intending to extract answers herself

### Journey to Estate

- Day passes; party arrives and plans overnight stay
- **[[Ronan]] asks [[Elara Sunstride]]** for her assessment of the situation
- **[[Ronan]] also mentions** a conversation with [[Richard]] on the mountain

### The Assassin Arrives

**Next Morning**

- [[Kaz]] alerts [[Elara Sunstride]] and [[Atasche]]: "bad vibes" stranger approaching
- **Appearance:** Plain gentleman, short dark hair, unremarkable features, "darkness" in his eyes
- **Claims:** Name is unpronounceable/unimportant; here to eliminate a target (not their concern)
- **Demonstration:**
    - Transforms Ronan into a skeleton/nervous system (psychic damage, then reverts him)
    - Kills nearby guard, transforms into grotesque organic tree-creature with single eye and exposed organs
    - Misha also transforms into another monstrous form
    - **Party reactively transforms:**
        - **Elara:** Blurred figure of wind and energy, radiating hot desert air
        - **Alice:** Fire elemental form
    - **[[Atasche]] secures killing blow** (Elara was charmed during combat; she had downed Misha)
    - [[Elara Sunstride]] **cures [[Misha]]** to stabilize her
    - **Party survives**
