### Summary

The party escapes Vassalor as meteors rain down across the city. Between desperate rescue attempts, a charmed crowd, black fire burning in the manor, and a brutal encounter with a familiar armored figure, the session ends with a catastrophic shockwave leveling most of the city — and the party barely alive outside the gates.

---

### Session Events

#### Fleeing the Plaza

- Meteors striking throughout the city; an elevated roadway takes a direct hit, dozens clinging for survival
- Party splits to maximize rescue efforts
- **Elara summons her steed for the first time**, using it to assist in the rescue
- Figures in white robes appear, directing survivors toward safe routes

#### The Temple

- A temple along the southern route has broken doors; inside are wounded civilians and priests whose healing magic is flickering and failing
- **Laogys identifies the wards as nonfunctional** — the temple isn't safe, everyone needs to evacuate

#### The Charmed Crowd

- Party encounters a silent line of people moving with no emotion or urgency
- **Zennick identifies them as charmed**, whispering _"she calls us"_
- The party locates the dark-robed figure responsible and breaks their concentration
- Elara rounds up some of those freed — but the party cannot save everyone
- A bridge collapses from a meteor impact nearby

#### The Manor

- The manor is burning with **black fire**; people are visible inside, seemingly unaffected by the flames
- A meteor strikes the road ahead
- **Elara heads inside**, leaving her steed to continue directing civilians
- Cold emanates from the black fire
- Inside a hall, **4 robed figures charge the party**
- The one they incapacitate **bursts open with tentacles** and dies violently
- The figures were **burning evidence** — the only legible fragment Ronan recovers reads: _**"Phase 2 successful"**_

#### Magic Destabilizes

- On exiting the manor, the party notices a **strange destabilization in the magical atmosphere**
- **Elara is visibly glowing**
- Elara and Richard notice — too late — that the guards and priests directing people toward safe routes have something wrong; **shadows and strange shapes coalesce**, followed by screaming

#### Southern Push

- The party splits again: **Zennick, Richard, and Kaz** push ahead while **Elara, Ronan, Atasche, and Laogys** stay to help stragglers over rubble
- Another meteor impacts nearby
- **Atasche heals Ronan** — glowing runes appear on the wound, and their **source reads as divine**

#### The Gates — Final Encounter

- As the gates come into view, buildings have collapsed and **statues of the gods are crumbled**
- A figure descends from the edge of a temple to block their path:
    - Tall, broad-shouldered
    - Leather cloak, furs at the neck
    - Black hair with white streaks, gaunt face, sharp features
    - _**"So you survived then. Shame."**_
    - **Identified as the same figure who took Ronan's armor on the road**
- Combat — he is overwhelming; **multiple party members fall** during the attempt to escape
- **Elara attempts to hold him off and fails**

#### The Shockwave

- As the party tries to escape, the air destabilizes further
- The tip of a tree behind them **ignites with blinding light**
- The light **bursts across the city in a shockwave** — 350-foot radius, everything evaporated; the wave continues outward, leveling buildings
- **Vassalor is destroyed**
- The party is outside the gates — alive

---

### Open Questions

- What is **"Phase 2"** — and what was Phase 1?
- What caused the destabilization of magic throughout the city?
- Why was **Elara glowing** after leaving the manor?
- What is the source of **Atasche's divine healing runes**?
- Who or what is **"she"** — the entity the charmed crowd was walking toward?
- What caused the final shockwave, and was it deliberate?
