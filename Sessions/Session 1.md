## Arrival in Tiranos

A courier meets us upon arrival with a letter for Elara from Tamuel.

### Letter to Elara

> You stand at the edge Elara, not yet a lantern, not yet what I had hope to make of you, but near enough I can see it gleaming in you. There is courage in your heart that most mistake for certainty, don't let them, certainty is brittle. It snaps when truth shifts behind it, courage, courage bends and endures. You have the makings of something rare, light that thinks. Too many that serve the divine mistake obedience for wisdom, you've never made that mistake. You question, you listen, that's why I choose you. Why I taught you to see the line between faith and blindness. Do not mistake me I have never turned from the gods, my prayers still burn, my faith still shapes my days. But there are cracks in the mosaic Elara, you've seen them too. Though perhaps may not name them aloud, silence that lingers where the last oracle once spoke. Prophecies that contract their own truths. Patterns of fate that seem incomplete, as if someone edited the threads and left no seem. I do not call this heresy, I call it hunger, the kind only knowledge can feed. If the gods are perfection, then our questions cannot harm them, if they are not then you are right to ask. Lyceum of Tiranos will open its grates for you with the enclosed rite. Not as a pilgrim seeking seeking absolution but as a lantern seeking understandings. You'll find minds eager to impress you, other that will dismiss you outright. They will all be helpful to you in a way, learn their disciplines, steal their secrets, but guard your soul. Magic without purpose is vanity, knowledge without mercy is tryanny, let the light in you temper both. As fort he circle, the others will not see you as I did. They will call you unproven, untried, let them. Every lantern must first walk through the dark alone, before they are trusted to carry the flames aside another. When that time comes remembers this, you may not lead by command, lead by example. The circle has too many voices and too few listeners, be the ear in that noise. If you dream of me, and you will, you'll hear my voice in the silence between stars. I will not tell you what to do, I will only remind you of what you already know. Light is not meant to chase shadows but to give them shape. Carry that truth with you. The world is not perfect, nor are the gods. But perhaps through you they might remember what perfection was meant to be. When the time comes, seek the heart of the Lyceum, there is something, someone, who will know the rest of what I could not uncover, until then...

We witness light glittering across the city—not stars, but lanterns.

## Meeting with Darrin Holt

We're guided to an office where **Darrin Holt**, administrator of the Lyceum, greets us. He informs us we are the last of Tamuel's students. His voice carries a trace of wariness.

> "Know this, the Lyceum of Tiranos does not thrive on sentiment nor legacy. Here your name only carries as far as your work can cast it's shadow. Though I suppose, the children of noble houses might tell you otherwise. You will find merit and influence often contest the same chair at our tables."

### House Selection Process

- **First week:** We will not belong to a house
- **Demonstration:** Show discipline and talent
- **Result:** Receive invitations from the five houses
- **Warning:** Choose carefully—your house will define more than your education, shaping alliances, rivals, and perhaps destiny

Holt advises us to keep Tamuel's name (and the Circle) close to our chests.

> "May your Minds Prove Sharper than Your Pride"

## Orientation: The Five Houses

### House Banners

- **Caelus:** Silver on blue with a soaring hawk
- **Virel:** Green on white with a blooming branch
- **Noctis:** Black on violet with a mirror of shadow
- **Solane:** Gold on crimson with a radiant flame
- **Ferren:** Brass on gray with an anvil and quill

### House Principles & Leaders

**House Caelus** - _Majester Thelren Voss_

> "Magic is a ladder not a chain, each rung is mastery. Each ascent a proof of will, the world bows not to those who wait for faith but those who grasp it!"

- Believes magic is the purest showcase of mortals
- Values heirs to legacy

**House Virel** - _Lyra Mornvale_

> "The brightest minds burn quickly and less rooted in compassion. Grown, endure, renew, we build not towers of glass but living legacy"

- Believes knowledge without compassion is a lie
- Values creativity and emotional depth

**House Noctis** - _Archivist Lume_

> "We walk between truths, between what is said and what is hidden. The mirror does lie but neither does it forgive"

- Truth revealed through what is hidden
- Values cleverness and secrecy

**House Solane** - _Sister Arathen_

> "Light is not comfort, it is revelation. The brave seek it, the wise endure it. We do not choose our purpose, we become it."

- Values courage, truth, and inspiration
- Belief that knowledge must be shared
- Embodies spirit and purpose

**House Ferren** - _Professor Dean Curos IV_

> "Ideas are cheap. Labor is divine. You'll earn your house colors by callus not cleverness. if you cant hammer theory into practice you don't belong here"

- Greatness is built, not bestowed
- Creation is the purest form of devotion
