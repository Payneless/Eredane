## Session 20 — _The Weight of Surviving_

**Date:** 5/24/2026 
**In-Game Date:** 32nd of Mishra, 2187 
**Start Location:** Outside [[Vassalor]] 
**Objective:** Survive

---

### Summary

The party regroups after barely surviving the Ikon of Necromancy — the city is gone, and the emotional toll is immense.

---

### Key Events

#### The Aftermath

- Short rest outside the ruins
- Elara in a state of utter defeat — outmatched, survivors saved but not enough

---

#### The Ikons Arrive

Two divine figures approach with fury:

|Name|Description|
|---|---|
|**Tylus**|7ft, shimmering robes, gold-spun hair, amber glowing eyes|
|**Nephy**|Ethereal, silver, moon-phase eyes, large longbow|

**Others present:**

- **Malena** — healing nearby survivors
- **Unknown** — twin-sun eyes, overcome with grief, staring at the ruins
- **Fortuna** — hanging at the edges, observing

Elara explains the situation. Tylus calls out **Akmera** — no response.

> _"He goes too far. What manner he has of circumventing his oath I know not — he's either complicit or has no control. Something will be done about this."_ — Tylus

Elara informs Fortuna that **the Ikon of Agrasyn was present.**

---

#### Reunions & Intel

- **Misha** confirms Lord Aerthyn survived
- **Bram (Ikon of Agrasyn)** was only dealing non-lethal damage — noted
- **Lord Aerthyn** assumes Sable Dominion is primarily at fault; flags possible **Silver Theocracy involvement**
- **Ronan** raises a hunch about the **Ikon of Akmera** — suggests a power imbalance or something wrong within the shadow domain's divine hierarchy
- **Kaz** speaks with Elara about **Shadall** — Elara holds her position on not engaging, given the domain's nature and suspicion

---

#### Varyn Ket's Offer

Varyn Ket rides out to speak privately with the party.

**Intelligence shared:**

- A **rogue/splinter faction** within the Dominion is acting outside known plans
- Info sourced from **high up** within the Dominion
- Believes there is **purpose** behind events — not random chaos
- **Umbris involvement** suspected — being used as a red herring / obfuscation
- Dominion members spotted in the area

**One-time offer extended to the group.**

Elara asks Varyn Ket a question **under Zone of Truth** regarding the Lantern's Circle being the priority.

---

#### Ronan's Theory

- Made contact with **Akmera**
- Believes a **third party** is orchestrating events, with Umbris positioned to reap the reward
- _"It doesn't matter why — it matters who"_
- Seeks to use **the mirror** on Elara

---

#### Group Vote — Varyn Ket's Offer

|Character|Decision|
|---|---|
|Elara|❌ Against|
|Atasche|❌ Against|
|Richard|❌ Against|
|Zennick|❌ Against|

Varyn Ket accepts the answer and suggests the party stay uninvolved going forward. He shares a rumor: **the Empire is moving east, into the Steppes.**

---

#### Other Observations

- Acolytes of **Tether** performing funeral rites nearby
- In Nemaris: a **young Aasimar teenager** spotted — half-blood children of gods; possibly connected to **Atasche and the glowing runes on his head**
- **Sirocco** — name of Varyn Ket's steed

---

#### Session End

Party **teleports back to Avenne.**
