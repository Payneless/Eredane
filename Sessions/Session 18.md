- Claims knowledge about Lord Drellis from [[Marodae]]

- In [[Marodae]], enchanted songs were being broadcast
- **Purpose:** Brainwashing/Memory alteration
- **Effect on party:**
    - Songs successfully altered memories for most party members
    - Erased certain events; reordered others
    - Magic that affects Atasche normally doesn't work (magic immunity)
- **What happened:** Party confronted the perpetrators; more arrived with additional memory-altering magic
- **Result:** Party's memories corrupted; Atasche's memories untouchable but made "unbelievable" to others

- **Elara takes 20 psychic damage** from the revelation
- Spell breaks partially; Elara no longer compelled to dismiss Atasche's claims
- **However:** Elara still cannot remember the actual events of Marodae
- **Divine Sense:** Cannot detect magic in Elara's mind; no detection of enchantment/abjuration effects

- **Next action:** Searching for someone skilled in enchantment/abjuration magic to undo the charm
