# Captain Meliora Strann

## Lesson 1 - Discipline & Foundation

### Opening Statement

> "Discipline is the only difference between a hero and a hazard"

### Initial Assessment

- All first-years are untested
- **Exception:** Looks directly at Elara with recognition of prior experience

---

## Training Structure

### Divided Groups

- **Casters** - Training on one side of the battlegrounds
- **Martials** - Training on the other side

### Elara's Focus

Plans to master accuracy through striking practice, drawing on her elven heritage and natural precision. Training single strikes rather than demonstrating full combat techniques (notably not using Ventosus style).

---

## Battlegrounds Information

- **Access:** Open 24/7 for independent training
- **Restrictions:** Duels discouraged but protective measures in place
- Implies students can use the space outside formal lessons
