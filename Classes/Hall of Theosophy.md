## Overview

The pantheon of gods is vast and covers many different aspects of existence. This lesson focuses on the **Arcanum** and **Shadow** domains to start.

---

## Ikons

**Definition:** Individuals who accomplish great things and are chosen by gods. Upon selection, they are granted a portion of the god's power to act as a force for that god within the mortal realm.

**Context to Arcanum Domain:** The original lower gods of Arcanum were all Ikons who showed exceptional promise in their respective schools. When mortals demonstrate similar expertise, they are granted boons by their domain's patron god.

---

## Arcanum Domain

**Upper God: Akros**
- Creator of magic itself
- Mortals tap into his creation when casting arcane spells
- **Form:** Ageless and ancient, appears differently to different observers
  - To young scholars: Appears as a figure with glowing arcane scripts, strands of light for hair
  - To seasoned mages: A weathered figure covered in endless runes traveling across his body
- **Philosophy:** Invested significant time and research into teaching mortals how to harness the arcane
- **Purpose:** Without his creation and guidance, raw arcane magic would be a destructive tempest within the world

### Lower Gods (The Six)

**Aegis**
- Domain: Abjuration
- Specialty: Defense and protection

**Agryn**
- Title: Spellfire Reaver
- Domain: Evocation
- Nature: Temperamental, commands far more powerful magicks than mortals can typically handle

**Flux**
- Domain: Transmutation
- Nature: Whimsical and tricky, unpredictable

**Iena**
- Domain: Enchantment
- Specialty: Confidence, subtlety, charisma
- **Important:** Often confused with Seraphel (goddess of love)
- **Warning:** Does not appreciate being confused with other goddesses; her visage shifts to match what each person perceives as beauty

**Reval**
- Title: Seer of Infinite Paths
- Domain: Divination
- Nature: Eerie to some, calming to others
- Connection: Tied with gods of fate

**Zyrin**
- Domain: Conjuration
- Status: The most recently added lower god
- Note: The principles governing conjuration create specific restrictions on its practice

---

## Shadow Domain

**Balance Point: Akmera**
- Role: Acts as the balancing force for the rest of the Shadow Domain
- **Important distinction:** Many mortals consider the entire Shadow Domain inherently evil; Akmera himself is not
- Nature: Intelligent, sharp, and acutely aware of his role
- **Philosophy:** Holds back certain aspects of the domain for purposes not fully disclosed

### Shadow Domain Deities

**Gehenna**
- Title: The Weeping Queen
- Domain: Misery
- Nature: Does not actively aggress; instead, invites wallowing in sadness
- Sustenance: Feeds off negative emotion
- Behavior: Attaches to those she influences, clingy

**Shadall**
- Title: The Hollow Silence
- Alignment: True neutral
- Nature: Completely emotionless and desireless
- Status: Held at bay by Akmera for unstated purposes

**Umbris**
- Title: Maw of the Abyss
- Alignment: Most commonly identified as evil
- Origin of demons
- Nature: Hunger incarnate
- Form: Quadrupedal, serpentine, with a rotating maw filled with teeth
- **Akmera's perspective:** Claims Umbris serves a purpose, possibly preparing for another divine war

---

## Demons

### Appearance & Behavior
- Will appear as normal mortals
- Capable of speech and communication
- Seek to entice and make deals
- Primary tactic: Deception to enable taking, destroying, or devouring

### Doctrine on Encounters
- **Sister Erethane's directive:** "If you see a demon, you should lop their head off"
- **Critical warning:** Do not believe their words under any circumstances

---

## Key Takeaways
- [ ] The pantheon is organized into upper and lower gods by domain
- [ ] Ikons are mortals elevated to serve divine purposes
- [ ] Arcanum domain emphasizes creation and guidance; Shadow domain emphasizes balance and hidden purpose
- [ ] Demons are inherently deceptive and should be eliminated without hesitation
