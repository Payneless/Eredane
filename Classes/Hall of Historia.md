# Edran Morn - History Amphitheater

## Lesson 1 - Divine War & The Covenant

### Setting

Large amphitheater lined with bookshelves. **Edran Morn**, historian and instructor.

---

## The Divine War of the Fateful

### Early History

- **Timeline:** Exact dates murky; events predate recorded civilization
- **Creation:** Gods created this world and all its civilizations
- **Growth:** Civilizations thrived and expanded; divine followers grew numerous
- **Conflict:** Competition for power and control emerged among mortal nations

### Escalation & Divine Intervention

As mortal wars intensified, followers of different gods clashed directly. Gods were eventually forced to intervene directly in mortal affairs. However, their intervention only **strengthened** mortal resolve for conquest—escalating rather than resolving conflict.

### End Date

War concluded in **1561**

---

## The Covenant of Restraint

A binding vow imposed on all gods with two primary restrictions:

1. **No god shall claim a crown or rule a mortal throne**
2. **Intervention is permitted only in catastrophic circumstances to save lives**

### Nature of the Covenant

- Described as "quite powerful"
- Magically binding on all divine entities
- Represents a fundamental restructuring of god-mortal relations

---

## The Period of Rebuilding

From 1561 to present day, civilizations have progressed under this new order. Focus shifted to:

- Mortal nations building independent power
- Personal and national advancement without divine rule
- Study of the past to prevent recurrence of divine war

---

## Current State & Threats

### The Sable Dominion

- **Status:** Name is known, but operates quietly and secretively
- **Nature:** Shadow government operating in the margins
- **Instructor's Warning:** "We shall see how long their time in the shadows lasts?"

### Divine Presence Today

- Gods still walk the world and interact with mortals
- Some adventure alongside mortals; others remain distant
- Gods do intervene, but strictly within the bounds of the Covenant
- Divine interference is permitted only under catastrophic circumstances

---

## Class Purpose

To study history not as academic exercise, but as practical lesson: understanding the past prevents its recurrence.
