## Quick Reference

**Title:** City of A Thousand Wells  
**Status:** Capital of [Skuviri Dominion](Skuviri%20Dominion)  
**Geography:** Fed by Rivers of Life (Dathir & Ma'jan) and vast subterranean aquifer

## Key Locations

**Palace of the Twin Flames**

- Where [Lexorr](Lexorr)'s light and [Nephy](Nephy)'s shadow meet in ritual
- Seat of [Sahira al-Namaris](Sahira%20al-Namaris)

**Wellkeeper's Guild Headquarters**

- Oversees all wells and water stewardship

**Military Quarter**

- Legions of Flame and Dust barracks
- Elara served in city guard here for decades

## Culture

- Sprawling city with trade districts
- Thousand Wells nickname from extensive water infrastructure
- Home to retired weapons masters
- Mix of military, religious, and mercantile influences
