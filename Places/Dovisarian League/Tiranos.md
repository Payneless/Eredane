

## Quick Reference

**Nickname:** The Spire City  
**Focus:** Education and magical advancement  
**Key Institution:** The Tiranos Lyceum of Applied Thaumaturgy and Arcane Philosophy

## The Lyceum

**Full Name:** The Tiranos Lyceum of Applied Thaumaturgy and Arcane Philosophy
- **Founded:** 1723 (464 years old)
- **Origin:** Offshoot of Arcane Collegia of Vaelion
- **Status:** Well-maintained but not as prestigious as Vaelion
- **Leader:** Rector Darrin Holt (Lantern's Circle member)

## Noble Houses of Tiranos

### House Thellan
**Role:** Innovation & Crafting
- Innovators' Guild
- Hold patents like others hold land
- Custom: Grant knighthood through invention
  - **Knight-Artisan** title
  - Earned through work advancing the League

### House Derrois
**Role:** Education & Licensing
- Lords of the Spire Collegium
- Education oversight
- Magical licensing
- **Houselet Osselan**
  - Oversee education in smaller League cities

### House Ventarre
**Role:** Artifice & Constructs
- Weaponry and construct creation
- Many dwarves in house
- Nuanced construction work
- Military applications

### House Irmalin
**Role:** Arts & Experimentation
- Eccentric house
- Art, illusion, alchemy
- **Notorious parties**
  - Famous across League
  - Sometimes lethal

## Significance

Education center of Dovisarian League. Houses focused on innovation, magical study, and artistic experimentation. Connection to Lantern's Circle through Rector Darrin Holt.
