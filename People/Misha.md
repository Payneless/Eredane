# Misha

## Core Identity

Protector of [[Avenne]] | Follower of [[Flora]] | Not aligned with any noble house

## Appearance & Demeanor

Taller woman with long blonde hair and piercing green eyes. Somewhat uncomfortable in fine clothes at the gala - more accustomed to practical attire. Carries a **strong celestial presence** detectable by Divine Sense.

## Role & Allegiances

- Less tied to the noble houses, more tied to the city itself
- Functions as a protector of [[Avenne]]
- Follower of **[[Flora]]** (deity)
- Aware of and concerned about unnamed organizations operating in the area

## Key Dialogue (Session 14)

- Certain organizations that will not be named have not acted like this before - the fact they're acting now is confusing and concerning
- Also troubled by the events at the Lyceum
- Seeks to continue gathering information to see if there is more at play
- Suggested that a meeting of **the Concordium** may be in order
- Noted that if the council was involved, it could be connected - seems strange unless certain unnamed parties are involved

## Current Status

Met at the House Aerthyan gala. Status following the attack and soul siphoning is unknown - she was not specifically accounted for during the evacuation.
