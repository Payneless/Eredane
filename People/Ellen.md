# Ellen

**Year:** 4th  
**Race:** Elf  
**Specialization:** Mirror magic and illusions  
**Focus:** Reflective focus

---

## Background

Family has a long tradition of mirror artistry. Ellen did not initially choose this path—it was inherited from her family lineage. Over time, she has come to embrace and specialize in the craft.

---

## Key Experience

One day, Ellen looked into a mirror and saw something that appeared to be her, but wasn't. The nature of this encounter is unclear, but it was significant enough to deepen her commitment to mirror magic studies.

---

## Current Status

Approaching final year at the Academy. Recently approached Elara's group with interest in their activities.

---

## Intentions

Ellen wants to meet the rest of Elara's group. She was introduced to Richard first and has expressed desire for broader connection with the party.

---

## Notes

- Potential connection to the reflection phenomenon occurring around the Academy
