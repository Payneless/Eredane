# Xotena - The Order Keeper

## Overview

Xotena governs the Law/Order Domain, embodying justice, civilization, progress, and the structured advancement of society.

## Domain & Lower Gods

**Law/Order Domain**
- **[[Irasis]]** - Justice
- **[[Cedrus]]** - Progress
- **[[Curos]]** - Engineering
- **[[Tinker]]** - Crafting
- **[[Ehrasil]]** - Civilization

## Power & Influence

The **2nd most powerful god by Ikon count**, with immense influence over matters of law, justice, and societal progress.

## Irasis & The Redemption Philosophy

[[Irasis]] is followed by [[Aequorin]], mentor to [[Elara Sunstride]], who espouses redemption and rehabilitation through justice. This philosophy heavily influences how Elara approaches morality and has shaped her path toward atonement.

## Divine War of the Faithful

Participated in the war (ended ~800 years ago, lasted 500 years)
- Bound by vow to not claim crown or mortal throne
- Now exists without direct mortal interference
- Intervenes only for existential threats
