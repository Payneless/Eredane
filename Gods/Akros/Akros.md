# Akros - The Arcane Master

## Overview

Akros governs the Arcane Domain, embodying magic, knowledge, transmutation, and the fundamental forces of spellcraft.

## Domain & Lower Gods

**Arcane Domain**
- **[[Aegis]]** - Abjuration
- **[[Zyrin]]** - Conjuration
- **[[Reval]]** - Divination
- **[[Iena]]** - Enchantment
- **[[Agryn]]** - Transmutation
- **[[Flux]]** - Transmutation

## Power & Influence

Ranks **#5 in pantheon hierarchy** but holds extreme significance among magic practitioners. All arcane domain lesser gods were originally created as [[Ikons]] to embody specific schools of magic.

## Divine War of the Faithful

Participated in the war (ended ~800 years ago, lasted 500 years)
- Bound by vow to not claim crown or mortal throne
- Now exists without direct mortal interference
- Intervenes only for existential threats
