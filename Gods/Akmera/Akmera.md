# Akmera - The Shadow Master

## Overview

Akmera governs the Shadow Domain, embodying darkness, the void, and the suffering found in the abyss.

## Domain & Lower Gods

**Shadow Domain**
- **[[Umbris]]** - Abyss
- **[[Shadali]]** - Void
- **[[Gehenna]]** - Misery

## Power & Influence

One of the **most secretive gods** in the pantheon. Rarely invoked or discussed openly, with minimal public worship.

## Divine War of the Faithful

Participated in the war (ended ~800 years ago, lasted 500 years)
- Bound by vow to not claim crown or mortal throne
- Now exists without direct mortal interference
- Intervenes only for existential threats
