# The Weaver - The Fate Keeper

## Overview

The Weaver governs the Fate Domain, embodying destiny, chance, time, and the threads of causality.

## Domain & Lower Gods

**Fate Domain**
- **[[Fortuna]]** - Chance
- **[[Eras]]** - Time

## Notable Lore

The **Oracle of Fate** vanished in 2071, an event that deeply haunts [[Tamuel Markath]]. The disappearance remains one of the great unsolved mysteries of the age, plaguing those who study fate magic and divine prophecy.

## Divine War of the Faithful

Participated in the war (ended ~800 years ago, lasted 500 years)
- Bound by vow to not claim crown or mortal throne
- Now exists without direct mortal interference
- Intervenes only for existential threats
