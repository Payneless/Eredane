# Animos - The Life Giver

## Overview

Animos governs the Life Domain, embodying vitality, healing, fertility, love, and the bonds between living things.

## Domain & Lower Gods

**Life Domain**
- **[[Malena]]** - Healing
- **[[Radona]]** - Fertility
- **[[Seraphel]]** - Love
- **[[Eradyn]]** - Hearth
- **[[Avzol]]** - Beast

## Power & Influence

The **3rd most powerful god by Ikon count**, with immense influence over life, healing, and the preservation of living things.

## Divine Relationships

Close relationships with [[Zephyr]] (air) and [[Seraphel]] (love), creating natural alliances among the pantheon.

## Divine War of the Faithful

Participated in the war (ended ~800 years ago, lasted 500 years)
- Bound by vow to not claim crown or mortal throne
- Now exists without direct mortal interference
- Intervenes only for existential threats
