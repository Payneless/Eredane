# Kethris - The Death Keeper

## Overview

Kethris governs the Death Domain, embodying mortality, rest, transition, and the abyss beyond life.

## Domain & Lower Gods

**Death Domain**
- **[[Seris]]** - Grave
- **[[Tethun]]** - Transition
- **[[Agrasyn]]** - Necromancy
- **[[Umbris]]** - Abyss (most hated lesser god)

## Philosophy

Seen as **balance to the Life Domain**, not inherently evil or opposed. Death is natural and necessary.

## Necromancy & Modern Understanding

Once strictly forbidden during the Divine War era, necromancy is now viewed with pragmatism. Modern practices like the **Ashen Concord** (followers of Gray Flame) demonstrate that necromancy can balance life and death when used with restraint and respect for the deceased.

## Divine War of the Faithful

Participated in the war (ended ~800 years ago, lasted 500 years)
- Bound by vow to not claim crown or mortal throne
- Now exists without direct mortal interference
- Intervenes only for existential threats
