# Tylus - Lord of Light

## Overview

Tylus is the most powerful deity in the pantheon by Ikon count, ruling over the Light Domain which encompasses the sun, moon, and artistic expression.

## Domain & Lower Gods

**Light Domain**
- **[[Lexorr]]** - The Sun Father (Dawn)
- **[[Nephy]]** - The Moon Huntress (Dusk)
- **[[Sennac]]** - Art

## Power & Influence

**Most Powerful God** - Highest number of Ikons
- Ranks #1 in pantheon hierarchy
- Immense influence across mortal realms
- Heavy worship focus, especially sun and moon aspects

## The Curse of Separation

Tylus bound Lexorr and Nephy to eternal separation, creating the cycle of day and night.

### Lexorr's Version
Long ago, Lexorr and Nephy stood together as guardians of Light's balance â€” the twin orbs of day and night. But the jealous trickster [[Khinir]] (Deceit) whispered a lie into their hearts: that only one of them could shine at a time, lest the world burn or freeze. In a desperate attempt to protect creation, **Tylus bound them to this cycle**, cursing them to eternal separation â€” never together, always chasing. Thus was born the eternal dance of sun and moon. 

Some myths whisper that if they were to meet again in the sky, the world would end in fire and shadow. Others say their reunion would bring about a golden age.

### Nephy's Version
When Lexorr was bound by Tylus' curse, Nephy was caught in its mirror. She is doomed to chase him across the heavens, never to catch him. Mortals believe the waxing and waning of the moon reflect her closeness to him â€” full when she nearly reaches him, new when she has fallen behind. Eclipses are the moments she looses her arrow of longing, brushing against him for the briefest of embraces before they part once more.

## Worship Traditions

**Lexorr** - Celebrated with hymns of heat and sacrifice at dawn
**Nephy** - Celebrated with time of prayer at dusk

Heavy focus on sun and moon worship, particularly in [[Skuviri Dominion]].

## Connection to Elara Sunstride

- [[Daliya Sunstride]] (adoptive mother) serves Lexorr in Ar'shahm temple
- Elara raised in Lexorr's faith but never felt called to it
- The "Sunstride" name carries Lexorr's blessing
- Elara found Zephyr instead, but respects her family's devotion

## Divine War of the Faithful

Participated in the war (ended ~800 years ago, lasted 500 years)
- Bound by vow to not claim crown or mortal throne
- Now exists without direct mortal interference
- Intervenes only for existential threats

## Top Ikon

The Ikon of Tylus is considered one of the three most powerful Ikons in existence.
