# Tylus - Lord of Light

## Overview

Tylus is the most powerful deity in the pantheon, governing the Light Domain which encompasses the sun, moon, and artistic expression.

## Domain & Lower Gods

**Light Domain**
- **[[Lexorr]]** - Sun (The Sun Father)
- **[[Nephy]]** - Moon (The Moon Huntress)
- **[[Savnac]]** - Art

## Power & Influence

**Most Powerful God** - Highest number of Ikons in the pantheon, giving Tylus unparalleled influence across the mortal realms.

## The Curse of Separation

Tylus bound [[Lexorr]] and [[Nephy]] to eternal separation, creating the cycle of day and night following [[Khinir]]'s deception.

### Lexorr's Version
Long ago, [[Lexorr]] and [[Nephy]] stood together as guardians of Light's balance â€” the twin orbs of day and night. But the jealous trickster [[Khinir]] (Deceit) whispered a lie into their hearts: that only one of them could shine at a time, lest the world burn or freeze. In a desperate attempt to protect creation, **Tylus bound them to this cycle**, cursing them to eternal separation â€” never together, always chasing. Thus was born the eternal dance of sun and moon.

Some myths whisper that if they were to meet again in the sky, the world would end in fire and shadow. Others say their reunion would bring about a golden age.

### Nephy's Version
When [[Lexorr]] was bound by Tylus' curse, [[Nephy]] was caught in its mirror. She is doomed to chase him across the heavens, never to catch him. Mortals believe the waxing and waning of the moon reflect her closeness to him â€” full when she nearly reaches him, new when she has fallen behind. Eclipses are the moments she looses her arrow of longing, brushing against him for the briefest of embraces before they part once more.

## Worship Traditions

**[[Lexorr]]** - Celebrated with hymns of heat and sacrifice at dawn
**[[Nephy]]** - Celebrated with time of prayer at dusk

Heavy focus on sun and moon worship, particularly in the [[Skuviri Dominion]].
