# Nyliah - The Trickster

## Overview

Nyliah governs the Trickery Domain, embodying deception, stealth, espionage, and cunning manipulation.

## Domain & Lower Gods

**Trickery Domain**
- **[[Myst]]** - Stealth
- **[[Savros]]** - Espionage
- **[[Khinir]]** - Deceit (most hated lesser god)

## Power & Influence

The **most secretive god** in the pantheon. Rarely discussed openly but maintains significant shadowy influence through networks of deception and espionage.

## Notable History

Participant in the **War of Painted Masks** (2105-2113), a shadow war against [[Savros]] with no recorded body count. The true nature and outcome of this conflict remain obscure.

## Khinir's Infamy

[[Khinir]] is one of the most hated lesser gods, responsible for whispering lies into the hearts of [[Lexorr]] and [[Nephy]], causing their eternal separation. This deception led to their cursing by [[Tylus]].

## Divine War of the Faithful

Participated in the war (ended ~800 years ago, lasted 500 years)
- Bound by vow to not claim crown or mortal throne
- Now exists without direct mortal interference
- Intervenes only for existential threats
