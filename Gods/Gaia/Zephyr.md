# Zephyr - The Breath of Change

## Overview

Zephyr is the divine embodiment of wind and change, one of [[Gaia]]'s children. Known as the Breath of Change, Zephyr represents adaptation, freedom, and the constant motion of air itself.

## Alignment & Personality

**Chaotic Neutral** - Though this alignment is less about personality and more about Zephyr's fundamental being:
- Serene and adaptive
- Elusive but approachable
- Rolls with the flow
- Becomes a raging storm when upset
- Never still but never chaotic
- Free and unbound

## Relationships with Other Gods

### Close Allies
- **[[Neptis]]** - Water and wind flow together naturally
- **[[Animos]]** - Shared appreciation for life and movement
- **[[Seraphel]]** - Unknown specifics but positive relationship

### At Odds
- **[[Oria]]** - Respects her but has fundamental disagreements
- **[[Pyre]]** - Work well together despite Pyre being very chaotic

## Worship & Followers

### General Perception
- Pretty chill deity
- Not heavily followed
- Most approachable of Gaia's children
- No active cause or grand plans
- Simply exists and flows

### Common Followers
- Sailors (who depend on favorable winds)
- Farmers (who need wind for pollination and weather)
- Travelers and wanderers
- Those seeking change or freedom

### Temples & Clergy

**Sacred Spaces**
- Open-air sanctuaries
- Built on cliffs, hills, and towers
- Places where wind flows freely
- No enclosed worship spaces

**Clergy: The Skywardens**
- Priests and priestesses who tend to Zephyr's worship
- Likely nomadic or semi-nomadic
- Focus on reading weather and interpreting the wind's guidance

**Symbols**
- Spiral of air
- Feather drifting on wind
- Circling swallows
- Colors: white, sky blue, pale gold

**Sacred Animals**
- Swallow (freedom and return)
- Hawk (soaring and vision)
- Butterfly (transformation)
- Horse (speed and wildness)

## Divine Philosophy

Zephyr doesn't impose will or demand worship. Instead:
- Guides those who listen to the wind
- Offers subtle pushes in new directions
- Helps redirect lives toward better paths
- Never forces, only suggests

## Divine Intervention

Zephyr's interventions are subtle:
- A sudden breeze drawing attention
- Wind shifting to warn of danger
- Stillness that marks significant moments
- Currents that guide ships or travelers

## Notable Followers

### Paladins
[[Elara Sunstride]] - Oath of Vengeance paladin who interprets her oath as redirecting others toward righteousness. She swore her oath during a moment when the wind completely stopped, and Zephyr's presence was unmistakable.

Her oath: *"I pledge my life in your name, Zephyr, and I vow to represent the Winds of Change. My life was changed, and I credit that to you. I wish to be the swift wind that redirects others into a proper and fulfilling life."*

## Elara's Understanding

From Elara's perspective, Zephyr:
- Saved her by guiding the caravan to find her in [[Qara'sul]]
- Calls to her through instinct and wind patterns
- Guided her to her mentor [[Aequorin]]
- Brought her to [[Tamuel Markath]]
- Led her to [[Richard Mortis]], [[People/Zennic]], and [[People/Atasche]]

The wind doesn't make mistakes--it simply redirects.

## Divine History

Participated in the **Divine War of the Faithful** (ended ~800 years ago, lasted 500 years):
- Bound by the divine vow to not claim crown or mortal throne
- Now exists without direct involvement in mortal affairs
- Will only involve if:
  - Multiple kingdoms are affected
  - Existence of world is threatened
  - Something approaches divinity

## Rituals: 
The Breath Prayer: Worshippers release a whispered plea into the wind, believing Zephyr will carry it where it must go. 
The Sky Lanterns: At festivals, paper lanterns are released into the wind as offerings, symbolizing freedom and release. 
The Gale Oath: A vow spoken atop a cliff or in the open air, sealed by the winds themselves.
    
## Myths & Legends 
The Whisper of Lovers: Zephyr once carried the words of two separated lovers across leagues of desert, reuniting them through the wind’s whispered song. The Storm of Freedom: When a tyrant sought to cage his people, Zephyr stirred a gale that shattered his fortress, scattering its stones to the four corners. 
The Last Breath: In ancient times, when the first mortal died, it was Zephyr who bore her final breath away.
## Nature of Zephyr's Divinity

Unlike gods with grand plans or causes, Zephyr simply *is*:
- The wind exists whether worshipped or not
- Change happens whether acknowledged or not
- Freedom flows regardless of recognition
- Adaptation occurs naturally
