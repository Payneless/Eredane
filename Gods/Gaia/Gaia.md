# Gaia - The Wild Mother

## Overview

Gaia governs the Nature Domain, embodying the primal forces of earth, air, fire, water, and all growing things.

## Domain & Lower Gods

**Nature Domain**
- **[[Oria]]** - Earth
- **[[Zephyr]]** - Air
- **[[Pyre]]** - Fire
- **[[Neptis]]** - Water
- **[[Flora]]** - Plant

## Zephyr - Most Approachable

[[Zephyr]] is the most approachable of Gaia's children, embodying a serene and adaptive personality. While generally chill and not heavily followed, Zephyr has close connections to [[Animos]] and [[Seraphel]].

## Notable Followers

[[Elara Sunstride]] follows Gaia as her patron deity, despite her family's devotion to [[Lexorr]]. This choice reflects her connection to nature and freedom over rigid hierarchical religion.

## Divine War of the Faithful

Participated in the war (ended ~800 years ago, lasted 500 years)
- Bound by vow to not claim crown or mortal throne
- Now exists without direct mortal interference
- Intervenes only for existential threats
